# Retro Future Smart Home Project

What is this? 
A beginner’s hands-on project and reflections by a college student majoring in IoT. A smart home system based on mechanical structures, microcontrollers, and edge computing.

Why am I doing this? 
Because I oppose “smart just for the sake of being smart.” Technology should ultimately serve people, not the other way around.

A few principles: 
Use mechanical structures instead of touchscreens whenever possible; in most smart home scenarios, tapping a screen with your finger is often counterintuitive. 
Rely on local processing rather than the cloud whenever possible. 
Automate tasks rather than relying on user input whenever possible. 
Keep things simple—don’t overcomplicate them with complex rules. 
And: smart technology is meant to lower the barrier to entry, not increase the learning curve.

## This is merely an experimental project from my college days. Many of the ideas are still in their early stages and have not yet been fully implemented.
